The reason I am writing: my pipeline produces a 3D head mesh from a non-defaced T1w MRI volume (marching cubes on the iso-surface), and the result has two unusual properties that no public face dataset matches at once:
- **No hair** — the T1 iso-surface stops at the skin/scalp, so the head looks like it is wearing a tight cap
- **Closed eyes** — subjects are supine in the scanner

I render this MRI-mesh from 9 frontal viewpoints and want a template-correspondence layer over them so the same anatomical point on different subjects ends up at the same UV coordinate. My specific questions:

1. **Topology assumption** — does MATCH require the input to come from the same FLAME-topology template, or can it consume multi-view renders of an *arbitrary* triangle mesh? In my case the mesh is generated by marching cubes, not parametric.

2. **Closed-eye supervision** — your training data presumably has open-eye supervision. If I gave you my 5 multi-view renders per subject with closed eyes, would the attention block degrade gracefully, or would it fail to register the periocular region?

3. **General advice** — if you were writing a paper that needs *one* fast, robust photo→mesh-or-mesh→mesh registration baseline, would you pick MATCH for its speed, or recommend something simpler given the closed-eye / no-hair edge case?

In exchange I would be happy to share 5 paired (MRI-derived mesh, photo) examples — that is a niche bit of data that you and Paulo / Siyu / Timo might find useful for benchmarking robustness.
