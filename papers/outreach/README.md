## How to use

1. Open the draft for the recipient.
2. Fill the `TODO: personalize` block — see the **Your turn** section in each file. Roughly 5–10 sentences in your voice, about who you are and your specific anatomical/methodological question.
3. Remove the TODO comment, paste subject + body into your mail client, send.

## Convention

- One topic per email — no multi-paragraph requests
- Lead with what you're building, follow with the specific question
- Offer something back (test set, finding, citation) when possible
- All correspondence in English (per global rules)
