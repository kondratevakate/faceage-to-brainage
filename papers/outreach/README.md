## How to use

1. Open the draft for the recipient.
2. Fill every explicit identity, affiliation, sponsor and ethics field truthfully.
3. Send from an institutional address where possible and record the exact text,
   date and follow-up date in the tracker.

## Convention

- One topic per email — no multi-paragraph requests
- Lead with what you're building, follow with the specific question
- Offer something back (test set, finding, citation) when possible
- All correspondence in English (per global rules)
