# Twin data-access campaign

**Status:** first-wave messages drafted; nothing has been sent.

**Tracker:** [`data/twin_dataset_outreach.csv`](../../data/twin_dataset_outreach.csv).

## Purpose

The first contact is a feasibility check, not a broad request for all available
data. It asks holders for an overlap count, data dictionary and consent/access
constraints before a full concept proposal is prepared.

## First wave

1. RATSS: direct paired medical facial photographs plus multimodal MRI.
2. Lithuanian Twin Centre/LSMU: large DNA-typed 3dMD cohort with repeat captures.
3. TwinsUK: test linkage between legacy 3dMD surfaces and the MRI campaign.
4. Forsyth/AAOF: test whether longitudinal records preserve twin pairing and
   cross-modality visit linkage.
5. TwinsMX: establish raw T1 facial coverage and possible linked face imaging.

### Eye-brain add-on

1. EMIF-AD PreclinAD: request source fundus/OCT files and raw MRI for the 67
   complete MZ pairs already used in a retinal-MRI analysis.
2. Monash T2D-discordant twins: verify retention and access for original OCT,
   retinal photographs and multimodal MRI in 35 people / 18 pairs.

Use the dedicated
[RetinaAge-BrainAge feasibility concept](retina_brain_data_access_concept_note.md)
for these two requests.

Second-wave targets are Danish LSADT, QTIM/QTwin, Osaka, Notre Dame 3D-TEC and
the Australian teeth-and-faces cohorts. Move a target forward only after a
current custodian and a working route have been verified.

## Contact cadence

| Day | Action |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Send one specific email or institutional contact form. Record the exact sent text and date. |
| 7 business days | Short follow-up in the same thread with the three key questions repeated. |
| 14 business days | Final follow-up asking whether another custodian is more appropriate. |
| After a positive reply | Offer a 15-minute feasibility call and send a one-page concept note. |
| After 30 days without response | Mark dormant; use one referred or public alternate contact, then stop. |

Do not cold-call personal numbers. A public institutional research number is
used only after email/contact-form outreach or when the page explicitly invites
telephone enquiries.

## Seven feasibility questions

1. Are stable family, zygosity, participant and visit IDs available?
2. For MRI, is original T1 available before defacing/skull stripping?
3. Does the field of view cover forehead-to-chin, and may it be inspected?
4. Does consent/DUA permit facial surface reconstruction, age estimation and
   derived embeddings without re-identification?
5. Which face/photo/3D/dental modalities overlap the MRI or longitudinal cohort
   at participant and visit level?
6. Are exact acquisition date, chronological age and repeated visits available?
7. Are model evaluation/training, derived landmarks/meshes and aggregate
   publication permitted in a controlled environment?

## Fifteen-minute call agenda

```text
00:00-02:00  Research question and why this cohort is uniquely suitable
02:00-05:00  Participant/visit overlap and age range
05:00-08:00  Image inventory, raw-versus-defaced status and FOV

```
