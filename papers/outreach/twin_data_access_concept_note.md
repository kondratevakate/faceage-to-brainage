# Feasibility concept: FaceAge and BrainAge in twin imaging cohorts

**Investigator:** Ekaterina Kondratyeva, `[institution/department]`

**Status:** feasibility concept only. Access, institutional sponsorship, ethics
approval/determination and funding are not yet implied and must be completed for
each holder.

## Research objective

The project asks whether frozen facial-age and brain-age pipelines make accurate,
repeatable and calibrated measurements in independent people. Only after those
two measurements are separately validated will a new registration test whether
their age-adjusted residuals are associated within the same participants and
twin families.

The work is methodological. It will not infer identity, diagnose a participant,
or treat either residual as a causal biological-age rate.

## Requested feasibility information

No images are requested at the feasibility stage. We ask for an aggregate
participant-by-modality count, age range, visit structure, data dictionary and
the applicable consent/DUA restrictions.

For a later approved analysis, the minimum variables are:

| Domain | Minimum data |
|---|---|
| Linkage | Random family, participant and visit IDs; zygosity where available |
| Time | Age and acquisition date or their permitted precision |
| FaceAge | Source standardized photograph or metric 3D face surface; capture and QC metadata |
| BrainAge | Original T1 MRI; scanner/protocol and QC metadata; raw/defaced status |
| Adjustment | Sex, recruitment/diagnostic stratum, site, BMI and selected health/lifestyle covariates agreed with the holder |

## Analysis sequence

1. Freeze model versions, preprocessing, QC, exclusions and model-training
   overlap ledgers.
2. Submit separate secondary-data preregistrations for external FaceAge and
   BrainAge validation before target predictions or accuracy outcomes are seen.
3. Report all attempted participants, failures, MAE, calibration and
   repeatability with family-clustered uncertainty.
4. If both branches are suitable, submit a third registration before opening
   the linked face-brain association outcomes.

All relatives, visits and images from one participant/family remain grouped.
Target labels are never used to select models, crops, preprocessing or bias
corrections.

## Data governance

Processing will occur only in the holder-approved environment. Direct
identifiers and linkage keys are not requested. Facial images, surfaces and
non-defaced MRI will not be redistributed. Re-identification and identity
matching are prohibited. Publications will contain aggregate results, failure
counts and disclosure-controlled illustrations only with explicit permission.

The study team will follow the holder's ethics, withdrawal, deletion, review,
cost and authorship requirements and will provide an institution-specific
security plan before transfer or remote access.
